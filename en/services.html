CNS & billing

How reimbursement works

Physiotherapy prescribed by a doctor is reimbursed by the CNS. Here is what you need to know.

  • Your prescription states the type of treatment, the number of sessions and their frequency
  • We bill the CNS directly (tiers payant) — you generally only pay your share
  • The CNS covers 70% of the tariff as standard — the rest is your co-payment (or your complementary insurance’s)
  • Treatment must start within 3 months of the prescription date
  • One reimbursable session per day (respiratory rehabilitation may add a second)
  • Home-visit travel fee only if medical necessity is noted, within Luxembourg

100% coverage & good to know

  • 100% covered for children and teens under 18 at the time of prescription
  • 100% covered for severe conditions listed in Annex G of the CNS statutes
  • 100% covered for post-surgical rehabilitation — up to 32 sessions
  • 100% covered for post-partum pelvic floor rehabilitation — up to 8 sessions
  • Every session includes at least 20 minutes of one-to-one treatment time
  • Please cancel at least 24h before your appointment — otherwise the full session is billed

Osteopathy is not part of the CNS nomenclature. Not sure which rate applies to you? Ask your therapist.
