Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Controller

The controller for data processing on this website is MOVEO Belair, 21 Rue Astrid, L-1143 Luxembourg, info@moveo.lu, +352 661 300 324.

Overview

This website provides information about our practice. It has no forms and no patient accounts. No health data is collected through this website. Appointments are booked through external providers (see below).

Hosting

The website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the date and time and the page requested in order to deliver and secure the website. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Google Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click "Accept" in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to understand how our website is used – for example which pages are visited and where visitors come from. This sets cookies and processes data such as device and browser information, approximate location and usage behaviour. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 4(3) of the Luxembourg law of 30 May 2005). Without your consent, no analytics cookies are set; Google then only receives cookieless, non-identifying signals (Google Consent Mode). Data is kept for no more than 14 months. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Withdrawing consent

You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page, or here: open cookie settings. Withdrawal applies from that point on.

Google Maps

The home and contact pages include an embedded Google Maps map. When the map loads, your IP address is sent to Google, and Google may set cookies. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing you how to find the practice (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information: Google privacy policy.

Google Fonts

We use Google Fonts for typography. When the page loads, your IP address is sent to Google. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a consistent presentation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Booking and external links

Treatments are booked through Doctena (doctena.lu) and classes through book.moveo.lu. Data you enter there is covered by that site's privacy notice. The same applies to other linked websites.

Contact by email or phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we use your details only to answer your request and delete them once they are no longer needed for that, unless we are legally required to keep them.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise them, email info@moveo.lu. You can also lodge a complaint with the Luxembourg data protection authority: Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), cnpd.public.lu.
